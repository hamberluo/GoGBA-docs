Save Point

Episodes

  • Save Point 001: Late-Night Commute

    Lin Xing's first "disconnect" happens at 11:47 PM on the last train home.

  • Save Point 002: Another Round

    Lin Xing plays again at home. A shadow NPC says "what you save becomes real." The plastic cup appears on the table.

  • Save Point 003: Origin Unknown

    Lin Xing investigates the ROM's source—it seems to have appeared from nowhere. He clears Stage 2; the cup becomes a notebook.

  • Save Point 004: Anonymous Post

    Lin Xing finds an anonymous forum post describing the exact same experience. He sends a DM: "I've had it too. Can we talk?"

  • Save Point 005: DM Echo

    The poster replies. They agree to meet—at a "safe" place. Lin Xing goes, wary of identity and safety.

存档点

剧集

  • 存档点 001：深夜通勤

    林醒第一次「断线」发生在末班地铁上，深夜十一点四十七分。

  • 存档点 002：再开一局

    林醒在家重开游戏。剪影说「存下来就会变成真的」。塑料杯出现在餐桌上。

  • 存档点 003：来源不明

    林醒调查 ROM 来源——宛如凭空出现。通关第二关后，杯子变成笔记本。

  • 存档点 004：匿名帖子

    林醒在冷门论坛发现匿名帖，发帖人描述的体验与他完全一致。他发私信：「我也遇到了。能聊吗？」

  • 存档点 005：私信回音

    发帖人回复，约在某处见面。林醒赴约，对安全与身份充满戒备。
