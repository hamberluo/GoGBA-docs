A quiet, reliable, and immersive GBA game emulator

Experience classic Game Boy Advance games like Pokémon, Fire Emblem, and Golden Sun with optimized touch controls, seamless saves, and a distraction-free interface. Designed for immersive gaming—open and continue playing instantly.

Key Features

Seamless Experience

Open and continue playing instantly. Your saves are always safe and never lost.

Reliable Save System

Automatic saves every 10 seconds with atomic writes. Exit anytime without worry.

Dark Themes

10 carefully designed dark themes (default: Catppuccin Mocha) for eye comfort during long sessions.

Clean UI

Minimal, distraction-free interface with transparent virtual buttons (10%-100% opacity).

Optimized Touch Controls

Normal and Arcade control modes with haptic feedback. Smooth, responsive touch experience.

Gamepad Support

Full controller support with customizable button mapping. Hide virtual buttons when connected.

RetroAchievements

Unlock achievements, track progress, and compete on leaderboards. Rich Presence support included.

Flexible Layout

Portrait or landscape mode with orientation locking. No unwanted screen rotation.

Speed Control

Adjust game speed from 1x to 3x. Optional audio muting during speed-up.

Video Filters

LCD, scanlines, bilinear, and HQ2x rendering filters for authentic retro visuals.

Multi-language

Support for 24 languages including English, Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, and more.

Game Library

Auto-scan game directory, favorite games, search, and cover management from libretro database.
